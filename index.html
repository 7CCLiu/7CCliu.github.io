About me

I received a Ph.D. in Computer Science under supervision of Prof. John C.S. Lui from CUHK in 2026. I received a B.S. in Statistics from USTC in 2022. Previously, I visited CUHK(SZ) in 2024, hosted by Prof. Zhi-Quan (Tom) Luo. My research focuses on optimization, and its applications in LLMs and quantum computing.

I will join Department of Artificial Intelligence, School of Engineering at Westlake University as an Assistant Professor in 2027. Please feel free to drop me an email if you want to work with me.

My publications can be found via Google Scholar.
